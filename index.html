OTTSub · for desktop Chrome

Two subtitle tracks. Your timing.

OTTSub is a free desktop Chrome extension that adjusts Netflix subtitle timing in 0.1-second steps and displays two subtitle tracks available for the title you are watching.

View OTTSub in the Chrome Web Store ↗

OTTSub is free. A Netflix account and subscription are required separately.

One panel, two independent tracks

Fine-tune each language

Adjust primary and secondary subtitle timing separately, in 0.1-second steps. Reset either track to 0.0s when needed.

Use the subtitles Netflix provides

Choose two available tracks for the current title. OTTSub does not generate translations or unlock missing languages.

Make room for learning

Choose subtitle size, color and background. Blur one or both tracks, reveal them on hover, or enable hover-to-pause.

Start with a video on Netflix

  1. Install on your computer

    Open the OTTSub Chrome Web Store page in desktop Chrome and install the extension.

  2. Open the side panel

    Play a video on the Netflix website. Click the OTTSub extension icon to open its side panel.

  3. Choose and adjust

    Select the primary and secondary subtitle tracks available for that title. Adjust their timing separately with the panel controls or shortcuts.

Subtitles 0.3 seconds late?

Starting from 0.0s, try +0.3s for the affected subtitle track to display it earlier. If it appears 0.3 seconds too early, try −0.3s instead. Listen, compare and fine-tune; a single offset may not fix a track that drifts over time.

Subtitles appear late+0.3sDisplay them earlier
Subtitles appear early−0.3sDisplay them later

Example values start at 0.0s. Primary and secondary offsets are independent. Adjusting subtitle timing does not change the video's playback speed.

Before you install

Can OTTSub show two languages on Netflix?

Yes, when both subtitle tracks are available for the current Netflix title and account. Choose the primary and secondary tracks in the side panel; their timing and appearance can be adjusted separately.

Does OTTSub translate subtitles automatically?

No. OTTSub displays subtitle tracks provided by Netflix. It does not create AI translations, provide a translation service, or add languages that Netflix does not offer for that title.

Why is my subtitle language missing?

Subtitle availability depends on the Netflix title and account. Check the languages Netflix provides for that title. Changing the OTTSub menu language does not add or translate subtitle tracks.

Are the extension and Netflix both free?

OTTSub itself is free and does not require a separate OTTSub account or subscription. A Netflix account and subscription are required separately and are not included.

Does OTTSub work in mobile Chrome, Safari or the Netflix app?

This package targets desktop Chrome on the Netflix website. It does not support mobile Chrome, Safari or operation inside the Netflix app. Being able to view this website on a phone does not mean the extension runs there.

What about Microsoft Edge and Mac?

Microsoft Edge on Windows has passed local compatibility tests. macOS has Option shortcuts and has been partially verified; complete Mac verification is pending. Other Chromium browsers have not been verified.

Can I choose ordinary subtitles or CC/SDH?

When the title provides them, OTTSub keeps ordinary subtitle and CC/SDH tracks separate. CC/SDH can include sound descriptions and speaker information. Track availability still depends on Netflix.

Does OTTSub send subtitles to an AI service?

No AI translation service is used. Subtitle tracks are retrieved from Netflix services and processed in your browser; preferences use Chrome storage. The store build excludes the local diagnostic recorder, analytics and viewing-history tracking tools. This does not mean the extension makes no network requests.

Is OTTSub an official Netflix product?

No. OTTSub is an independently developed extension and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Netflix.

OTTSub is offered by K2Bact in the Chrome Web Store. Netflix access is separate. This page loads without analytics scripts, account forms or external fonts.
